About

UNI-NAVIGATE is a web design project developed by the UNI-Team, a local group specializing in campus and architectural photography, The concept targets to provide a safe and knowledgeable campus experience through the college life with the partnerships to universities, to produce a. website"UNI-NAVIGATE"

            The UNI-NAVIGATE is a website extension through Learning Management System (LMS) , a campus navigation website leaning campus architecture to designated rooms use showcase . The website helps users avoid confusion with schedules, locate specific areas, and navigate the campus more efficiently.
